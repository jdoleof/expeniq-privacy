# Privacy Policy for ExpenIQ

**Effective Date:** September 28, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 28, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **ExpenIQ** (the "Application"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, access, or use ExpenIQ. ExpenIQ is designed to allow users to manage and track their personal expenses and financial information directly on their mobile devices.

**ExpenIQ does not collect, transmit, or store your financial or personal information on our servers.**

By using the Application, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Financial Information

ExpenIQ allows you to enter and manage information such as:

* Expense records
* Income records
* Expense categories
* Amounts
* Dates
* Descriptions and notes
* Other financial information that you voluntarily enter into the Application

This information is stored **locally on your mobile device** and is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by ExpenIQ or its developers.

### 2.2 Personal Information

ExpenIQ does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Mailing address
* Telephone number
* Government identification numbers
* Bank account information
* Credit or debit card numbers
* Social Security numbers
* Other personally identifiable information

We do not have access to the financial information you enter into the Application.

### 2.3 Usage and Analytics Information

ExpenIQ does not use analytics services, advertising tracking technologies, behavioral tracking tools, or similar technologies to collect information about how you use the Application.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage

All information entered into ExpenIQ is stored locally within the Application's data storage on your mobile device.

Because the information is stored locally:

* ExpenIQ developers cannot access your stored financial records.
* ExpenIQ developers cannot view the contents of your expense records.
* ExpenIQ developers do not receive copies of your financial information.
* Your information is not transmitted to our servers for storage or processing.

The availability and preservation of locally stored information may depend on your device, operating system, backups, or other device-level settings.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

ExpenIQ only requests device permissions that are necessary to provide the Application's intended functionality.

Any permissions requested by the Application are used solely for the purposes described within the Application or by the applicable mobile operating system.

ExpenIQ does not use device permissions to collect or transmit your personal or financial information to our servers.

You may be able to review, modify, or revoke Application permissions through your device's operating system settings, subject to the functionality and requirements of the applicable operating system.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

ExpenIQ does not intentionally use third-party services that collect, analyze, sell, or otherwise process your financial information.

The Application does not use:

* Advertising networks
* Advertising tracking SDKs
* Behavioral tracking services
* Third-party analytics services
* Third-party financial data collection services

However, the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and the operating system on your device are operated by third parties. Their collection and processing of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of Apple and Google for information regarding how those platforms may process information independently of ExpenIQ.

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because ExpenIQ does not collect or transmit your financial information to our servers, we do not sell, rent, or share your financial information with third parties.

We do not provide your financial records to:

* Advertisers
* Data brokers
* Marketing companies
* Financial institutions
* Analytics providers
* Other third parties

There may be information processed independently by Apple, Google, your mobile device manufacturer, or other platform providers as part of the operation of your device or the applicable app store. Such processing is outside the control of ExpenIQ and is governed by the applicable third party's privacy policies.

---

## 7. Data Security

ExpenIQ is designed so that your financial information remains stored locally on your device.

Because ExpenIQ does not maintain a centralized server containing your financial records, the security of your locally stored information depends in part on the security of your device.

We recommend that you:

* Use a strong device passcode or PIN.
* Enable biometric authentication when available.
* Keep your device's operating system up to date.
* Keep your device physically secure.
* Use only trusted applications and software on your device.

While we design ExpenIQ to protect locally stored information, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

---

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because ExpenIQ does not maintain copies of your financial information on our servers, we do not retain your financial records after they are deleted from your device.

You can delete information stored by ExpenIQ using the data-management or deletion functionality provided by the Application, where available, or by removing the Application and its associated local data from your device, subject to your device's operating system and backup settings.

If your device creates backups of Application data, those backups may continue to contain information until they are deleted or overwritten according to the backup system's policies.
